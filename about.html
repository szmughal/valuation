Most people come to us with a deadline and a form that has a box on it marked “value of assets”. What they actually need is not a number — it is a document that the embassy, the court, the bank or the tax authority will accept without argument.

That distinction shapes everything we do. Before we quote, we ask who is going to read the report, because a bank credit committee, a visa officer and a civil judge each want something different on the page. Then we inspect, gather comparable evidence from the specific locality, apply the method appropriate to the purpose, and set out our reasoning so it can be checked.

We are based in Karachi and know the city's submarkets in detail — the difference a corner plot makes in DHA, how Scheme 33 rates diverge street by street, what industrial land in SITE actually transacts at. That local depth is what separates a defensible valuation from a plausible guess. Outside Karachi we work through the same process in every major city in Pakistan.

The practice covers property, vehicles, gold and jewellery, stock and inventory, consolidated net worth statements, and back-dated valuations for tax and litigation. Whatever the asset, the standard is the same: show the evidence, state the method, sign the certificate.

How we work

Four commitments we do not trade away.

These are the things clients are really buying when they choose an independent valuer.

Independence

We are paid for the report, never for the number in it. If the honest figure is lower than you hoped, that is the figure you get — which is precisely why the report carries weight with the person reading it.

Evidence on the page

Every valuation shows its working: the comparables, their sources, the adjustments made and the assumptions relied on. A reviewer should be able to follow our reasoning without calling us.

Confidentiality

Ownership documents, financial statements and asset details stay between us and you. Nothing is shared, sold or reused, and files are held securely.

Speed without shortcuts

One working day is our standard because our process is organised, not because steps are skipped. If a case genuinely needs longer, we say so upfront rather than rushing a weak report.
