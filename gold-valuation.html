Jewellery is the asset most often questioned in a visa file, because most declarations carry no evidence behind the number. We test purity, record gross and net weight excluding stones, photograph each item, and value against the published Karachi Saraf rate on the date of inspection — giving a figure the reviewing officer can verify independently.

What this covers

  • Gold jewellery — 18k, 21k, 22k and 24k
  • Bullion bars, biscuits and coins
  • Diamond and gemstone-set pieces
  • Silver and platinum articles
  • Antique, heirloom and bridal sets
  • Inherited collections and estate lots

Common purposes

  • Visa and immigration asset declaration
  • Insurance cover and claim support
  • Inheritance division among heirs
  • Wealth statement and tax records
  • Sale, resale or pledge negotiation
  • Bank locker and safe-custody records

How we arrive at the figure

1

Purity assay

Karat verified item by item using electronic testing and touchstone acid comparison, cross-checked against any hallmark present.

2

Net weight capture

Gross weight recorded, then stone and setting weight deducted to establish the true gold content that carries the value.

3

Live rate application

Net weight priced at the Karachi Sarafa Bazaar rate for the inspection date, with the rate and date printed in the report.

What we need from you

  1. CNIC or passport of the owner
  2. The items themselves, for testing and weighing
  3. Original purchase receipts, if available
  4. Any existing certificates for stones
  5. A list of items, where the collection is large

Documents are used solely to prepare your report and are not shared with any third party.

Questions about gold & jewellery valuation

Do I have to bring the jewellery to you?

You can visit our office, or for larger collections we can arrange inspection at your bank locker or home. Testing is non-destructive — nothing is cut, melted or damaged.

Are diamonds and stones included in the value?

Yes. Stones are assessed separately from the metal, and the report shows both figures so the basis of the total is transparent.

How long does the certificate stay valid?

Gold moves daily, so the report is a snapshot of the inspection date. Most embassies accept a report up to three to six months old; for older files we can reissue at a reduced rate.
