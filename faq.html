Getting started

How quickly can I get my valuation report?

Standard property, vehicle and gold valuations are delivered within one working day of inspection. Net worth and stock reports take two to four working days because more evidence has to be gathered. If you have a filing deadline, tell us the date when you first call and we will confirm whether we can meet it before you pay anything.

Will embassies and courts actually accept your reports?

Yes. Our reports carry the valuer’s credentials, the method used, the comparable evidence relied on, dated photographs and a signed certification page — which is what makes a report defensible rather than just a number on a letterhead. If a reviewing authority raises a query, we respond to it at no additional charge.

What does a valuation cost?

Property and vehicle valuations start at Rs. 5,000, gold at Rs. 6,000, net worth reports at Rs. 12,000 and stock valuations at Rs. 15,000. The final figure depends on asset type, location and purpose. You get the exact fee in writing before any work begins, and it does not change afterwards.

What documents do I need to provide?

At minimum, your CNIC or passport and proof of ownership for the asset. The full checklist differs by asset — property needs the registry or transfer letter, vehicles need the registration book, gold needs the items themselves. Each service page lists exactly what is required.

Delivery & coverage

Do you charge extra for the hard copy or delivery?

No. Stamped hard copies are couriered anywhere in Pakistan at no extra cost, and the signed PDF is emailed as soon as the report is ready.

Can you value assets outside Karachi?

Yes. We cover Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Hyderabad, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar and Quetta as standard, and can arrange inspections in most other districts. Travel outside the standard coverage may carry a small additional charge, which we confirm upfront.

Is a physical inspection always necessary?

For built property, vehicles and jewellery, yes — a value defended without inspection is easy to challenge. For open plots in documented schemes and for back-dated valuations we can often work from records and archived market evidence. We will tell you which applies to your case before you commit.

Validity & payment

How long does a valuation report remain valid?

There is no fixed expiry, but most embassies and banks want a report dated within three to six months of submission. Markets move, so an older report simply carries less weight. We can reissue a previous report at a reduced rate if your circumstances have not changed.

Can you provide a valuation for a past date?

Yes — that is our back-date valuation service. We reconstruct the value as at a specific historical date from archived transaction records, official rate notifications and period market data, citing every source in the report.

How do I pay, and do you issue a receipt?

Bank transfer, cash or online payment. A receipt is issued for every payment, and the fee is confirmed in writing before work starts.

Service-specific questions

Property Valuation: Is a site visit always required?

For built property we strongly recommend it, and for bank and court work it is mandatory. For open plots in a documented scheme we can often value from records and locality evidence. We will tell you which applies to your case before you pay.

Vehicle Valuation: Do you need to see the vehicle?

For a defensible market value, yes. We can inspect at your home, office or workshop anywhere in Karachi, usually within the same working day.

Gold & Jewellery Valuation: Do I have to bring the jewellery to you?

You can visit our office, or for larger collections we can arrange inspection at your bank locker or home. Testing is non-destructive — nothing is cut, melted or damaged.

Stock & Inventory Valuation: Will you shut down my warehouse to do this?

No. We schedule around your operations, count in sections, and can work evenings, weekends or during a scheduled stock freeze.

Net Worth Report: Can you include assets held abroad?

Yes, where you can evidence ownership and value. Foreign assets are listed separately with the currency and conversion rate shown.

Back-Date Valuation: How far back can you go?

Routinely 20 to 25 years for property in documented schemes, and further where registry or society records survive. Tell us the date and we will confirm what evidence exists before you commit.

Still stuck?

Some cases genuinely do not fit a standard answer — disputed ownership, assets abroad, an unusual deadline. Call and describe it; there is no charge for the conversation.
