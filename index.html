How quickly can I get my valuation report?

Standard property, vehicle and gold valuations are delivered within one working day of inspection. Net worth and stock reports take two to four working days because more evidence has to be gathered. If you have a filing deadline, tell us the date when you first call and we will confirm whether we can meet it before you pay anything.

Will embassies and courts actually accept your reports?

Yes. Our reports carry the valuer’s credentials, the method used, the comparable evidence relied on, dated photographs and a signed certification page — which is what makes a report defensible rather than just a number on a letterhead. If a reviewing authority raises a query, we respond to it at no additional charge.

What does a valuation cost?

Property and vehicle valuations start at Rs. 5,000, gold at Rs. 6,000, net worth reports at Rs. 12,000 and stock valuations at Rs. 15,000. The final figure depends on asset type, location and purpose. You get the exact fee in writing before any work begins, and it does not change afterwards.

What documents do I need to provide?

At minimum, your CNIC or passport and proof of ownership for the asset. The full checklist differs by asset — property needs the registry or transfer letter, vehicles need the registration book, gold needs the items themselves. Each service page lists exactly what is required.

Do you charge extra for the hard copy or delivery?

No. Stamped hard copies are couriered anywhere in Pakistan at no extra cost, and the signed PDF is emailed as soon as the report is ready.

Can you value assets outside Karachi?

Yes. We cover Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Hyderabad, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar and Quetta as standard, and can arrange inspections in most other districts. Travel outside the standard coverage may carry a small additional charge, which we confirm upfront.
